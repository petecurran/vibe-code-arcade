# Welcome to your Vibe Code Arcade

This folder is where you and your children make games together. They describe
the game, out loud is best, and the AI builds it.

## Each time you play

1. Open this folder in your AI app (Claude or Codex). On Claude, the usual
   model (Sonnet) works well. On Codex, choose GPT-6-Luna: it's quick, and
   children don't like waiting.
2. Let your child say what they want to make or change.
3. Play what it builds. Then they say what's next.

Try the five magic words: **"What questions do you have?"**

## What keeps things safe

- The AI has rules for this folder (in `AGENTS.md`): child-friendly content,
  no personal details, and it stays inside this folder.
- It can search the web for ideas and help. Before it downloads or installs
  anything, or touches anything outside this folder, it should stop and say
  **🔑 Ask a parent**. Your AI app will
  also ask your permission for these things. Read what it wants to do before
  you say yes.
- Nothing is perfectly safe. Stay nearby, and if anything looks odd, say no
  and ask the AI to explain.

## Where things are

- `games/`: one folder per game. Open a game's `index.html` to play it.
- `arcade/`: the arcade's notebook (lessons learned, and what you play
  with).

## Save points

The AI saves the games after every change that works, like autosave. If
something goes wrong, ask it to go back to an earlier save point.
